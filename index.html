Reinforcement Learning / Multi-Agent Systems

About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Ying Wen and co-advised by Yang Li.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning and multi-agent systems: how agents adapt to new tasks and coordinate with unfamiliar partners. I work on in-context reinforcement learning, zero-shot coordination, and human–AI collaboration.

Research Interests

  • In-context learning / in-context reinforcement learning
  • Human–AI collaboration and zero-shot coordination
  • Continual reinforcement learning
